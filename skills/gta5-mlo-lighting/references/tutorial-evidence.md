## Source-backed details and worked cases

- The [2.4.0 UI](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/blob/v2.4.0/ydr/ui.py#L144-L198), [creation operator](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/blob/v2.4.0/ydr/operators.py#L143-L158) and [light exporter](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/blob/v2.4.0/ydr/lights.py) show the old field bindings and XML behavior. The [2.9.1 UI](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/blob/v2.9.1/ydr/ui.py), [creation operator](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/blob/v2.9.1/ydr/operators/lights.py) and [light exporter](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/blob/v2.9.1/ydr/lights.py) show the current controls for this guide, recursive light collection and root-relative vectors. Different authoring fields can serialize to the same native light.
- The [tagged light presets](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/blob/v2.9.1/ydr/gta5/presets/light_presets.json) show why the initial default is not proof of a useful light. A wall preset gives a reproducible numerical starting point, but neither it nor an exporter fixture is a verified stock in-game fixture.
- The [shader definition](https://github.com/Sollumz/szio/blob/0789b450d4321c7c61268103fcf88e888e81b91e/src/szio/gta5/Shaders.xml#L490-L515), [Sollumz shader setup](https://docs.sollumz.org/tutorials/creating-props/setting-up-sollumz-shaders) and [interior texturing](https://docs.sollumz.org/tutorials/creating-interiors/texturing) support the native sampler, lens assignment, and texture packaging. The [CodeWalker light schema](https://github.com/dexyfex/CodeWalker/blob/485d56bec00262ed7fa472261cce7bbc6202b96e/CodeWalker.Core/GameFiles/Resources/Drawable.cs) and [capsule gizmo](https://github.com/dexyfex/CodeWalker/blob/485d56bec00262ed7fa472261cce7bbc6202b96e/CodeWalker/Rendering/Renderer.cs#L979-L1063) support inspecting the separate light records and their axes. Neither preview certifies the game result.
- The [2.9 worked asset guide](https://github.com/AlexJamesDean/sollumz-fivem-guide) illustrates selection-based export to each generation. The [Sollumz releases](https://github.com/Sollumz/Sollumz/releases) identify 2.8 as the start of supported native binary workflows. The [Cfx Enhanced docs](https://docs.fivem.net/docs/developers/legacy-vs-enhanced/#stream_enhanced-folder) specify resource-tree replacement, and [Alchemist docs](https://docs.fivem.net/docs/alchemist/) list supported conversions.
- [The closed-door case](https://forum.cfx.re/t/problem-with-interior-not-loading/5126770) identifies two particular flagged portals and a room that doesn't render while its door is considered closed. Check links and door state when the whole room disappears. Do not copy that flag indiscriminately. A [duplicate-name case](https://forum.cfx.re/t/help-renaming-a-mlo-ydr-file-and-setting-their-dependencies/1601289) reports breakage from renaming only a drawable. Trace all references instead. [The bright-at-night case](https://forum.cfx.re/t/mlo-problem/5225882) suggests vertex color or timecycle, without establishing a reproducible fix. Use that as a clue only after isolating direct light.

**Office status.** Dim lens and missing-looking wash are user reports. No project asset, export or FiveM result was independently inspected. The four-state test is the proof to run, not a result already obtained.
